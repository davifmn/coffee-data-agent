# Grão Fino marketplace policies (internal)

Grão Fino is a fictional specialty-coffee marketplace used for this demo. These are its
internal rules for partners, pricing and shipping.

## Partner farm requirements

- Every listed coffee must score at least 80 points (SCA) as assessed by a licensed
  Q Grader.
- Partners must keep an average customer rating of at least 4.0 stars across all their
  coffees. Partners below 4.0 are placed on a quality improvement plan and their listings
  lose homepage placement until the average recovers.
- Coffees must be shipped with the roast date printed on the bag and sold within 30 days
  of roasting.

## Commission and payouts

The marketplace takes a 12 percent commission on the product value of each delivered
order (shipping excluded). Payouts to partners happen every 15 days. Cancelled orders do
not generate commission.

## Shipping

Orders with a product subtotal of R$ 150.00 or more ship for free. Below that, customers
pay a flat shipping fee of R$ 18.90, R$ 24.90 or R$ 29.90 depending on the destination.
Orders ship within 2 business days.

## Returns

Customers can return unopened bags within 7 days of delivery (Brazilian consumer code
right of withdrawal for online purchases). Opened bags are replaced only when the coffee
arrived damaged or past the 30-day roast window.

## Sales channels

Orders come from the mobile app, the website and Instagram (through the shop link and
direct messages). Instagram campaigns started in 2026 to reduce customer acquisition
cost compared with paid search.
