# Brewing guide

## General rules

Use filtered water at 90 to 96 °C (just off the boil). Weigh coffee and water: a good
starting ratio for filter coffee is 1:15 to 1:17 (for example, 15 g of coffee for 250 g
of water). The SCA "golden cup" targets an extraction of 18 to 22 percent and a strength
of about 1.15 to 1.35 percent total dissolved solids. Grind just before brewing.

## Pour-over (V60, Kalita, Melitta)

Medium-fine grind, ratio around 1:16. Pour about twice the coffee's weight in water to
bloom for 30 to 45 seconds, then pour in slow circles. Total brew time of about 2:30 to
3:30. If it tastes sour, grind finer; if bitter, grind coarser.

## French press

Coarse grind, ratio 1:12 to 1:15, steep for 4 minutes, stir, skim the foam and plunge
slowly. Produces a heavy-bodied cup.

## Espresso

Fine grind, about 18 g of coffee yielding about 36 g of liquid (1:2 ratio) in 25 to 30
seconds at around 9 bar. Medium roasts are the usual choice for Brazilian espresso.

## AeroPress

Medium-fine grind, ratio 1:13 to 1:16, 1:30 to 2 minutes of steeping before pressing.
Forgiving and great for travelling.

## Cold brew

Coarse grind, steep 1:8 (concentrate) in cold water for 12 to 18 hours in the fridge,
filter and dilute to taste. Low acidity and naturally sweet; naturals and pulped
naturals from Brazil work very well.
